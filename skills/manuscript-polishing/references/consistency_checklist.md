# Consistency checklist

Write this table to `<project>/10-polish/consistency.md` and fill one row per term you had to choose.

| term | chosen form | abbreviation | defined in abstract | defined in main text | times used |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Rules:

- Use one name for each concept. The name is the one in the locked outline unless the journal's nomenclature page requires another. Record the switch as a `consistency` row in the change log.
- Define an abbreviation at first use in the abstract and again at first use in the main text. If you use it once or twice, spell it out and do not abbreviate it.
- Numbers: follow the journal's author page. If that page is silent, spell out one to nine and use numerals from 10, and write `Number style: project default, journal page silent` in this file.
- Species and gene symbols stay in markdown italics (`*Genus species*`) until the docx export.
- US or UK spelling follows the journal. Do not mix.
- `significant` stays only when an evidence row reports a statistical test and the sentence says so. Otherwise hedge or delete it, and log the edit.
