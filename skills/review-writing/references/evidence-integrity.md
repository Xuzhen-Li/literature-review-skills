# Evidence integrity

These rules override style. In a suite project they add to `shared/writing_rules.md` (evidence binding and claim strength ladder).

## Never

- Invent references, data, experimental conditions, or conclusions.
- Rewrite an association as a cause ("is associated with" does not become "drives").
- Widen a study's scope (one soil, one column, one cultivar, one season stays that).
- Drop units, time scales, treatment temperatures, or the order of treatments when they affect the meaning.
- Add a mechanism the cited studies do not support. A plausible mechanism without a source is either marked `[NEEDS SOURCE]` or written as a hypothesis with that label.
- Add a new reference unless the user authorized a search and the record was checked against the original paper or the publisher page.

## State design limits precisely

Name the actual limit rather than a vague caution: sequential treatments on the same sample, a single soil column, spiked rather than aged contamination, oven-dried samples, laboratory incubations versus field events, short time scales, one measurement method or operational definition.

## When evidence is thin

Narrow the claim. Do not hide the gap with vague wording ("may potentially", "could possibly"). Use the claim strength ladder: one study = suggestive; conflicting studies = contested, cite both sides.

## When merging studies behind one claim

- Check each cited study really supports that claim under the stated condition.
- If one study differs in condition or design, say so in the boundary clause instead of folding it in.
- Keep every citation that still supports a kept sentence. Removing the last citation of a kept factual sentence is not allowed.

## Metadata problems

Wrong year, volume, pages, or author capitalization in the reference manager are listed for the author to fix in Zotero. Do not "fix" them by editing the field JSON or guessing values; mark unknowns `[VERIFY]`.
